Brainstorming Frameworks

  1. Names first. Combine first names, last name, or initials — that's where puns live.
  2. List sound-alikes. Write every word your last name sounds like (Wright = right, Bell = bells).
  3. Plug into templates. #The{L}s, #{P}Here{L}Stay, #AlwaysAndForever{L}.
  4. Test uniqueness. Search Instagram; if it's crowded, add the year or city.

When Your Last Name Has No Pun

Not every name puns cleanly — and that's fine. Use first names (#SamAndJo2026), initials (#TeamNK), or the wedding city (#ChicagoSaysIDo). A clear, ownable tag beats a forced pun every time. The goal is a tag your guests will remember at 11pm, not a pun only you appreciate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I find wedding hashtag ideas?

Start from your names, then browse by style — funny, romantic, classic, or cute. Our idea hub gathers curated lists, and the free generator produces personalized tags from your last name.

How do I brainstorm a wedding hashtag?

Write your last name, list sound-alikes, then plug them into templates (#TheLs, #PHereLStay). Ten minutes of this beats an hour of staring at a blank box.

What if my last name has no good pun?

Use first names, initials, or the wedding city instead. #SamAndJosAYear, #TeamNguyen, and #ChicagoSaysIDo all work without a pun.

How many ideas should I pick from?

Generate 20-40, then narrow to one primary tag plus one backup. More than that splits your photo collection.

Narrowing Down to One

Once you have 20-40 candidates, cut to a shortlist by asking three questions: Can a guest spell it drunk? Does it sound like us? Is it already crowded on Instagram? Anything that fails a test gets dropped. You'll usually land on one clear winner plus a backup. The winner goes on every sign, the backup lives in the caption for variety. Resist the urge to use ten — a split collection is worse than a single strong tag.
