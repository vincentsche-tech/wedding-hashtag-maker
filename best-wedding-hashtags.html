Make a Trending Tag Your Own

A trending format only works if it points back to you. Take a loved phrase and swap in your details so it can't be confused with anyone else's day:

  • #CheersToForever → #CheersToTheWrights
  • #MrAndMrs → #MrAndMrsJohnson
  • #TieTheKnot → #TheSmithsTieTheKnot
  • #ForeverStartsNow → #ForeverStartsForTheBells

This keeps you on-trend while staying unique — exactly what the algorithm (and your guests) reward. Our free generator does the swap automatically the moment you type your names.

Common Mistakes That Make a Tag Forgettable

  • Too many words. #WrightAndSmithGetMarriedInTheGarden2026 will never be typed correctly.
  • No capitalization. #thewrightwedding is a lowercase blob; #TheWrightWedding reads cleanly.
  • Copying a viral tag. If a tag already has millions of posts, yours vanish.
  • Changing it later. A tag switched halfway through splits your photo collection in two.
