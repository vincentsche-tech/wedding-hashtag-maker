How to Make a Wedding Hashtag in 3 Steps

A great wedding hashtag makes it easy for guests to tag photos so you can collect every moment in one place. Here's how to create one:

  1. Start with your names. Combine your first names, last name, or initials — that's where the best puns live.
  2. Pick a vibe. Go punny and witty, romantic, short & classic, or cute. Match it to your invitation style.
  3. Check it's unique. Search the tag on Instagram and Pinterest before you print it on signs and favors.

Tips for a Memorable Wedding Hashtag

  • Keep it readable. CamelCase (#TheWrightWedding) beats run-together text so guests don't mistag.
  • Use your last name. Shared or new last names unlock the punniest options.
  • Make it specific. Add the year or wedding city if your name is common.
  • Test before you commit. A quick search avoids an accidental double meaning.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a wedding hashtag?

A short, unique tag (like #WrightSaysIDo) that you and your guests add to photos on Instagram, Pinterest, and TikTok so every moment collects in one searchable place.

Are wedding hashtag generators free?

Yes — this tool is completely free, requires no sign-up, and works instantly in your browser.

Should my hashtag include my last name?

If you're taking or sharing a last name, including it usually produces the cleverest, most personalized puns. Leave it blank if you prefer first-name-only tags.

Can I use the same hashtag on Instagram and Pinterest?

Absolutely. The same tag works across Instagram, Pinterest, TikTok, and Facebook — consistency helps you gather every photo in one spot.
